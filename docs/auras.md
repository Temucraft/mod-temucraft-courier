# mod-temucraft-auras (part of mod-temucraft-courier)

Cosmetic aura toys. Using one switches its aura on; using it again switches it off. Only one aura at a time.
The choice is saved per character and comes back at login and after death. The auras are stock look-only spells.

- Hidden in battlegrounds and arenas (`Temucraft.Auras.HideInPvP`), and in combat if `Temucraft.Auras.HideInCombat` = 1.
- Sold by the Temucraft Curator, Auras list (vendor 608010 from mod-temucraft-shop), for 100 or 125 tokens.
- In the Vanity window under "Auras" (mod-temucraft-account-wide).
- Items 610110 to 610137. Table `mod_temucraft_character_setting` (setting `auras`) in the characters database.
